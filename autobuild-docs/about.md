# About AutoBuild

## Our Mission

AutoBuild is an intelligent pipeline automation platform that simplifies workflows,
enhances collaboration, and accelerates project delivery — all while maintaining
clean design and user-friendly experiences. We aim to empower teams to focus on
innovation rather than manual tasks.
